folder. All are freely licensed (CC0, public domain, or CC BY) — sources
below. Files were trimmed and loudness-normalised when converted to MP3
for the site; the recordings themselves are otherwise unaltered.

**Kiss** has no file: no freely-licensed recording of a kiss sound could
be found (the candidates on Wikimedia Commons and SoundBible were speech
files or "personal use only"), so Kiss keeps the small synthesised
version generated in the browser. Every other sound above also keeps its
synthesised version as an automatic fallback if the file fails to load
or play.
