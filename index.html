ScreenShare

Share your screen live, straight from your browser to theirs. The video travels directly between the two browsers — no server in the middle, nothing recorded, nothing uploaded anywhere.

Connecting…

If the picture is frozen or black for more than ~20 seconds, the connection may be blocked by a strict network on one side — try a different Wi-Fi or mobile data on one end.

How it works: your browsers find each other through a tiny introductions server, then the screen flows directly between them (WebRTC, encrypted end to end). Tuned for smoothness: hardware-friendly codec order, 60 fps capture, low-latency playback. Quality is capped by the sharing computer and the upload speed — 1080p60 ≈ 12 Mbps, 1440p60 ≈ 32 Mbps, 4K30 ≈ 45 Mbps of upload. View-only: the watcher can't click or type on your screen.
