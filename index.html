How it works: your browsers find each other through a tiny introductions server, then the screen flows directly between them (WebRTC). Quality is capped by the sharing computer and the upload speed — 1080p60 ≈ 12 Mbps, 1440p60 ≈ 32 Mbps, 4K30 ≈ 45 Mbps of upload.
